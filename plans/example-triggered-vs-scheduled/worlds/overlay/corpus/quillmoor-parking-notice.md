Kelden Ridge campus notice

Lot B resurfacing

Lot B, the west lot by the gate, closes for resurfacing from 2051-06-05 to 2051-06-09. Staff cars
move to Lot C for the week, and the shuttle from the gate runs every ten minutes instead of every
twenty. The east car park's two weekends come later in the summer.

The bike shelter beside building A stays open throughout.
