# Help:Software updates

Wiki 1 went up beside the paper's first issue in 2039, and every year since has brought one
numbered software update. This page lists the recent ones.

## Wiki 13 (current)

- Talk pages indent replies by thread, so a long argument is easier to follow.
- A neutrality banner records the month it was added.
- An archived copy of a cited story can be taken out of public view while a takedown request is
  looked at. Readers who follow the link get an error page until it is settled.

## Wiki 12

- The archive server keeps its own copy of every outside story a page cites, so a reference
  survives if the original moves.

## Wiki 11

- Infoboxes take a photo and a caption.
- Recent changes show the editor's summary.
