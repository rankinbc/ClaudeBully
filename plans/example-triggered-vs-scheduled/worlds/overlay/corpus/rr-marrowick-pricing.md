RACK & RELAY WEEKLY | Monday, 16 January 2051

# Marrowick County rack rates hold as power pass-through climbs

By Juno Achterberg

Colocation list prices in Marrowick County held flat through the fourth quarter, but tenants paid
more anyway as the county's two operators passed on a winter rise in power costs.

A standard 5 kW cabinet still lists at about $1,150 a month on a three-year term. The metered
power charge on top of that rose 9% in November, and cross-connect fees went up $15 at one of the
two sites.

"The headline number hasn't moved, and that's what goes in the board slides," said Tilda Rensch, a
broker who places tenants in the county. "The bill moved."

Vacancy across the county sits near 11%, up from 8% a year ago, after a regional insurer
consolidated into a single site. Rensch expects at least one operator to offer a free month on new
two-year terms before spring.
