# Retirement notice: Quillmoor Fax Bridge

Fax Bridge has turned faxed support requests into tickets since 2033. After eighteen years it
closes at the end of this quarter, and the fax number will stop answering.

- Customers who still fax us can call the support line instead. Calls that reach voicemail are
  transcribed and answered within one business day.
- Tickets opened by fax stay open and keep their numbers.
- The last six faxes we received all came from the same customer, who has been told.

Questions go to support@quillmoor.example.
