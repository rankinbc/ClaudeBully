# About the Ledger

The Vessick County Ledger has covered Varrow Falls and the county townships since 1931. We print on
Thursdays and post most stories online the same morning.

**Staff.** Odile Marchbank, editor. Petra Vane, county reporter. One part-time photographer,
shared with the high school yearbook.

**Corrections.** We correct errors of fact on page two of the following weeks paper and at the
foot of the story online.

**Subscriptions.** Three stories a month are free online, and after that it is a subscription. We
know. We hear about it at the grocery store too.

**Letters.** Under 300 words, signed, with a town. We print nearly all of them.

**Back issues.** Stories from 2040 on are online. Older issues are on microfilm at the county
library, open Tuesdays and Saturdays.

12 Front Street, Varrow Falls. newsroom@vessickledger.example
