# Billing portal: what's new

Portal 1 replaced paper invoices in 2039, and the portal has had one numbered update a year since.
Portal 13 is the current version.

## Portal 13 (current)

- Statements download as one file per quarter.
- Paper statements can be turned off from the account page.
- Billing questions left on the support line's voicemail reach the billing team the same day.

## Portal 12

- Invoices show the hall that served each request.
- The account number appears on every page of a statement.

## Portal 11

- Card details can be updated without calling the support line.
