VESSICK COUNTY LEDGER | Thursday, April 20, 2051

# School board moves high school start to 8:40

By Petra Vane

The Vessick County school board voted 5-2 on Tuesday to start the high school day at 8:40 a.m.
from the fall term, twenty-five minutes later than it starts now.

Board chair Rosalind Ebbesen said the change follows a year of surveys of students and bus
drivers. "Nobody learns algebra at five to eight," she said. The two members who voted against it
said the later finish would cut into practice time for the spring teams.

The board also approved $186,000 to replace the gym roof over the summer, after leaks closed the
gym twice in March, and renewed the bus contract for three years with a fuel clause.

The next meeting is Tuesday, May 16, in the high school library. Public comment opens at 7 p.m.
