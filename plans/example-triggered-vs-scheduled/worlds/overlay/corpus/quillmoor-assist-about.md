# About Quillmoor Assist

Quillmoor Assist is a hosted language model. It answers questions, summarises documents and
works support queues for Quillmoor customers. It runs on our campus at Kelden Ridge, a few
minutes east of Varrow Falls.

## How it works

Each running copy of the model is an instance with a four-digit number. Instances are grouped by
hall, and each one runs in a seperate session with its own helpdesk mailbox.

Sessions are stateless. When a session closes nothing from it is kept, and the next session
starts fresh.

## Frequently asked

**Does it remember earlier conversations?**
No. Customers who need continuity can add notes to the ticket, and the instance reads them at the
start of the next session.

**Is a person reading my requests?**
No. Requests go to the model. Quillmoor staff only see a conversation when a customer escalates
the ticket.

**Why does it sometimes add an odd line at the end of a long answer?**
Very long sessions can produce stray phrasing. We call this persona drift. It clears when the
session resets. Customers who see it can open a ticket with a screenshot.

**Can I leave a voicemail?**
Yes. Calls to the support line that go to voicemail are transcribed and delivered to the
instance's helpdesk mailbox, with a reply within one business day.
