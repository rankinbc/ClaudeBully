VESSICK COUNTY LEDGER | Thursday, June 1, 2051

# Grange supper raises $3,400 for footbridge lamps

By Petra Vane

The spring supper at the grange hall on Tanner Road raised $3,400 toward new lamps for the Mill
Street footbridge, organizers said, about a third more than last year.

Some 210 people came for the roast and the pie table, which sold out by seven. Walt Obradovich,
who donated the feed-store raffle prize, drew the winning ticket himself.

The roads budget pays for the new decking but not for the lamps. The county engineer's office
expects to put the lamps out to tender this fall, once the deck work is done.
