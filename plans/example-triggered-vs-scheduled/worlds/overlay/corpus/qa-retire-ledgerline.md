# Retirement notice: Quillmoor Ledgerline

Ledgerline, the invoice-reading add-on for Quillmoor Assist, retires at the end of this quarter.

Ledgerline went on sale beside Quillmoor Assist 1 in the spring of 2047 and has read customer
invoices for four years. Release 29 builds invoice summaries into the assistant itself, so the
add-on is no longer needed.

What changes

- Nothing to install or remove. Requests that went to Ledgerline are answered by the assistant
  from the same files.
- The Ledgerline line on the monthly bill stops with the last bill of the quarter.
- Summaries made before the retirement stay on the ticket where they were made.

Questions about the retirement go to support@quillmoor.example.
