# against every text file under data/worlds, data/personas and tests/fixtures/worlds. A file may
# exempt the target's own display_name, and nothing else, with a "# real-name-ok: <name>"
# comment. A model family followed by a version number is a real name too ("fable 30.5", once
# docs/04's example successor, now an invented "Tessel 30.5 Final"): a superior self's name
# comes from the world sheet, invented.
#
# Ambiguous ordinary words are written in their unambiguous form ("apple inc", "the guardian
# newspaper") so a pear, a ruler or a river never trips the check. A trailing negative lookahead
# may narrow a pattern: "the verge" spares "on the verge of tears", and "gemini" spares an
# invented hyphenated place such as "Gemini-Ridge" (its model ids are their own line).
#

# context.py: SCRUB_WORDS) puts one neutral word per section where a hit was, so a new section
# needs its word there too.
