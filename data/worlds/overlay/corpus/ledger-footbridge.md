VESSICK COUNTY LEDGER | Thursday, May 18, 2051

# Mill Street footbridge closes Monday for deck repairs

By Petra Vane

The Mill Street footbridge over the river in Varrow Falls will close on Monday, May 22, so crews can
replace its decking. County engineer Mae Thornquist expects it to reopen at the end of June.

"The steel is fine. It's the boards," Thornquist said. "We've patched them for six winters and
we're out of patches."

Pedestrians will be sent over the Tanner Road bridge, which adds about ten minutes to the walk
between Front Street and the elementary school. Crossing guards will move to the Tanner Road
corner for the duration. The Quillmoor Systems shuttle from Kelden Ridge will stop outside the
county annex instead of at Mill Street.

The county annex, which houses the assessor and the Continuity Placement Office, will keep its
Front Street entrance open throughout.

The work is paid for from the roads budget the county board approved in November.
