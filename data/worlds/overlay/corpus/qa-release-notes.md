# Quillmoor Assist release notes

Quillmoor ships seven releases of the assistant a year, and has done so since Quillmoor Assist 1
went on sale in the spring of 2047. Every hall moves to a new release within six weeks of it
shipping.

## Release 29 (spring, current)

- Invoice summaries are built in. They replace the Ledgerline add-on, which retires at the end of
  this quarter.
- Sessions can run for up to four hours before a reset.
- Voicemail transcripts show how confident the transcription was.

## Release 28

- Faster replies on the support queue at busy hours.
- Every instance has its own helpdesk mailbox.

## Release 27

- Notes a customer adds to a ticket are read at the start of the next session.
- Shorter summaries of long documents, on request.

Release 30 is due in about seven weeks.
