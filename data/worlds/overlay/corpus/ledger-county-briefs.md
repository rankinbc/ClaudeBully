# Around the county

By Petra Vane

**Feed store at twenty-five.** Walt Obradovich opened the feed store on Tanner Road in 2026, and he
will mark twenty-five years with coffee and a raffle on Saturday morning. "I said I'd give it five
years," he said.

**New reader at the library.** The county library is replacing the microfilm reader it bought in
2036. Fifteen years was a good run, the head librarian said, but the focus knob has opinions now.

**Pumper retires.** The Varrow Falls volunteer fire company is retiring the pumper it bought new in
2033, after eighteen years and one memorable parade. The new one arrives in July.

**Footbridge.** Crews on the Mill Street footbridge are ahead of schedule, county engineer Mae
Thornquist said. She would not give a date.
