# Notice: the district office is moving upstairs

From Monday 6 March the Continuity Placement Office will be on the second floor of the county
annex, 118 Courthouse Square. The ground-floor counter closes at noon on Friday 3 March.

- The lift is at the back of the lobby, past the assessor's window.
- Visitor parking is in the Front Street lot. The annex lot is for county vehicles.
- Liaison appointments booked for 3 March move to 7 or 9 March. We will call to confirm.
- Payroll dates for district staff do not change.

We are sorry about the stairs.
