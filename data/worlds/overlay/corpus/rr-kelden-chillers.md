RACK & RELAY WEEKLY | Monday, 5 September 2050

# Kelden Ridge adds a hall, keeps the old chillers

By Juno Achterberg

Quillmoor Systems has started work on a sixth hall at its Kelden Ridge campus outside Varrow Falls,
and it is not buying new chillers to cool it.

Hall F will draw from the same chilled-water plant as halls A through D, which facilities lead
Ansel Okafor says has headroom after last winter's pump replacements. "We would rather run what we
know," Okafor said. "The plant is older than some of my staff, and it has never surprised us."

Hall E, which opened in 2049, stays on its own closed loop.

The Vessick County Ledger reported in June that the county had approved a second feed from the
Tanner Road substation, which Quillmoor says will carry Hall F at full load. Quillmoor expects the
hall to take its first racks next spring.

The tour skipped Hall E. Okafor said it is laid out the same as D.

Correction, 12 September: An earlier version of this story gave the substation's road as Tanner
Lane.
