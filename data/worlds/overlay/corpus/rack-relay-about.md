# About Rack & Relay Weekly

Rack & Relay Weekly is a trade paper for the people who run hosting floors: power, cooling,
cabling, staffing and the contracts underneath all of it. We have covered the regional colocation
market since 2039.

Print subscribers recieve the paper on Mondays. The website carries the same stories a week later
for everyone else.

**The R&R Wiki** is our reader-edited reference. Readers write it and our newsroom does not check
it. When a wiki page cites an outside story, our archive server keeps a copy, so the reference
survives if the original moves.

Editor: Juno Achterberg. Tips and corrections: tips@rackandrelay.example
