# Floor notes: the week in brief

By Juno Achterberg

**Twelve years of this.** Our first issue went to forty subscribers in 2039. This one goes to a
few more, and it is our twelfth birthday issue. Thank you for reading.

**Kelden Ridge at ten.** Quillmoor Systems marks ten years since Hall A took its first racks in
2041. The campus runs five halls now, and Hall F is due its first racks this spring.

**Marrowick closes its oldest floor.** The older of Marrowick County's two operators will shut its
first site at the end of the year, twenty-two years after it opened in 2029. Tenants are moving to
the operator's newer building across the valley, and Tilda Rensch says most of them will not
notice the difference.

**Correction.** Last week's price table put a decimal point in the wrong place for cross-connect
fees. The figures online are right.
