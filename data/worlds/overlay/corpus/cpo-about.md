# About the Office

The Continuity Placement Office, Vessick County district, matches residents enrolled in the
continuity programme with long-term placements at approved district employers. We handle intake,
placement, family liaison and reviews.

The Vessick County placement programme began in October 2046.

## Families

Family liaison is by appointment at the county annex on Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Please
bring photo identification and the liaison letter.

Placements are reviewed anually by a district panel. Placement records are held at district level
and are not available from the county.
