# Kelden Ridge campus notes

**Twenty years on the plant.** Ansel Okafor joined Quillmoor in 2031 to look after a single
chiller in a rented basement. Twenty years later he looks after the plant for five halls and says
the basement chiller was the best of them.

**Shuttle at ten.** The shuttle from Varrow Falls started with Hall A in 2041 and has run every
working day for ten years. While the Mill Street footbridge is shut it stops outside the county
annex.

**Trays.** The canteen would like its trays back.

**Car park.** The east car park closes for resurfacing over two weekends this summer.
