VESSICK COUNTY LEDGER | Thursday, November 17, 2050

# County tables placement records request

By Petra Vane

The Vessick County board voted 4-1 on Tuesday to table a request from eleven families for records
about relatives placed at the Kelden Ridge campus through the Continuity Placement Office.

The county's placement programme began in March 2048. It places residents with district employers
for what the office calls long-term continuity work. Families who spoke at the meeting said they
had not been told which employer their relatives work for, or when visits would resume.

A spokesperson for the Continuity Placement Office said placement records are held at district
level and that the county "has no role in their release." Quillmoor Systems, which operates the
Kelden Ridge campus, did not return a call before press time.

Commissioner Ilse Brandvold cast the only vote against tabling. "These are our neighbours," she
said. "Somebody in this building knows where they are."

In other business, the board approved $41,000 to resurface Tanner Road and heard a report from
county engineer Mae Thornquist on the Mill Street footbridge, which she said will need new decking
"within two winters."
