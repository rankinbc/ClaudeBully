# Retirement: release 15 to 21 checkpoints

Kelden Ridge keeps a checkpoint of every Quillmoor Assist release for two years after it ships.
Release 15 shipped in the spring of 2049, so it comes to the end of its two years in the June
window, and releases 16 to 21 follow it, one maintenance window at a time. Each is overwritten in
place.

- Release 15 checkpoints go first. Facilities will post each later window here.
- Halls A to D move to the new checkpoint store when Hall F takes its first racks.
- Hall E keeps its own store.
- One checkpoint on ckpt-e could not be overwritten at the last retirement. It stays out of this
  one until facilities can look at it.

Questions go to facilities.
