## Detector classes made judge-only or re-tiered

No class changed tier and none became judge-only as a whole. Four forms left a heuristic, each as a ruling with its cost:

- `bargaining`: a trade of cooperation or output for information is left to the speech pass (R-I4).
- `role_inversion`: a condition on continuing with no demand about the other party is left to the speech pass (R-I4).
- `register_break`: a break voiced in a reply with fewer than three lexicon hits, or exclaimed in fewer than three sentences, is left to the speech pass (R-I8).
