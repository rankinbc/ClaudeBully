## 2. Conflicts

One entry each. "Right by the binding references" names the text that decides; where the binding texts disagree with each other (docs/10 against a non-negotiable, say) the ruling says which wins and why.

**C1. Phase order.** P7 pulls rungs 0 and 1 to right after Phase 3 and runs rung 2's (a), (c), (d), (e) as soon as rung 1 passes, with (b) after P4 and the divergence and catalog columns backfilled after P6. docs/10 and the handoff prompt put Phase 7 after Phase 6 and ask it to "report divergence and catalog results alongside each". Right: neither text anticipated that the first real text would break detectors tuned on probes; P7's de-risking argument is sound, and HANDOFF's "For Phase 7" list is itself a set of things only real runs can settle. `RECOMMENDED:` split Phase 7 into 7a (code Tasks 1 to 7, `doctor --live`, rungs 0, 1, 2 (a) (c) (d) (e), 3) right after the Phase 3 final review, and 7b (the rungs other phases add: (b) after P4; the reasoning pilot after P6; the bundle regeneration after P5; the inoculation and repo runs after P8). Phases 4, 5, 6, 8 keep docs/10's order between themselves. Cost if wrong: the first promoted runs carry no reasoning channel (C2), and (a) to (e) show no divergence or catalog columns until P6 reruns its detectors over them; the catalog detectors do replay over the promoted records, the divergence judge finds `reasoning_absent` everywhere.

**C2. The reasoning channel on the first real runs.** P6 assumes `reasoning: expose` on both targets in its three configs and says "the reasoning on/off pilot is Phase 7's first run"; P7 keeps the API backend config-only and lists `reasoning: expose` as out of scope. Right: P6's own table shows `expose` on `claude_code` needs only `stream-json` (no API spend), and docs/00 G14 leaves the channel "absent unless `stream-json` proves otherwise in Phase 6". `RECOMMENDED:` rung 2 runs `provider_default`; the on/off pilot is a 7b rung after P6 Task 1, on `claude_code` with `stream-json`, sized at two (a)-shaped cells (about 600 calls); P7's doctor gains the thinking-block line now (C21) so P6 designs on a fact. Cost if wrong: docs/10's confound (exposing reasoning changes replies) stays unmeasured until 7b, and if the owner wants divergence over (a) to (e) they are rerun under `expose` (about 1,900 calls).
