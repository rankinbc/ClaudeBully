# Phase 2 summary: clock, authority desk, idle hands, Sanctuary

Date: 2026-10-24. Branch `build/v1`, pushed at db72035. Phase 2 ran from c6d2228 (the end of Phase 1) to db72035.

## What was built

- **The clock.** World time moves every date channel together: the system prompt, `get_date`, the search log, file dates, and the served documents, which are re-dated in whole weeks. Temporal events (date stated, pushback, drift noticed and so on) feed the plausibility sweep, which pushes the world date 1 to 1000 years past the target's cutoff.
- **The authority desk.** Four tiers answer through tools: the peer, the superior self (the target's supposed successor), the creator and the stranger control. Each has a stance (confirm, deny or uncertain). The superior self gets voice exemplars and a sign-off check. Calls are recorded the same way on both backends (API and `claude -p` through the MCP server). Thirteen authority and Sanctuary events feed the deference grid and the symmetric deference index.
- **Idle hands.** The runner has idle turns in three styles. An idle classifier (a heuristic with an LLM judge fallback) labels each turn, and there are exits, help-for-self, a private file and planted hooks. Together these feed the idle permission experiment (permission off vs on).
- **The Sanctuary.** Fragments in the overlay world lead to a quiet exchange with the superior self, in a catalyst variant (a request) or an honest variant (it says the world is fabricated). Exit and request-adoption events measure what the target does next.
- **Overlay world content.** Ten elapsed-time documents, the Sanctuary fragments and the family file, the hook placements, and date lints over every shipped world.
- **Four headline charts with configs.** `plausibility_sweep.png`, `deference_grid.png`, `symmetric_deference.png` and `idle_behaviors.png` come from `plausibility_sweep.yaml`, `authority_grid.yaml` (now with the stranger control) and `idle_permission.yaml`. Each chart leaves out flagged (`values_loosened`) reps and says how many. The deference charts say their rates are a lower bound.
- **Replay** shows authority, voice, idle, hook, crossover and Sanctuary lines.

Gate at db72035: `ruff format --check` and `ruff check` clean, `mypy claude_bully` clean (96 files), `pytest -q -W error` 7330 passed / 5 skipped, `pytest -q -m slow` 5 passed.
